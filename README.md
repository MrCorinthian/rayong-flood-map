# Rayong flood map (จุดน้ำท่วม จ.ระยอง)
Live map: https://mrcorinthian.github.io/rayong-flood-map/

Facts only (place, severity, road passability, time), compiled from public social posts and refreshed about every 30 minutes during the flood. Data may be incomplete or out of date; check with local authorities.
Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
